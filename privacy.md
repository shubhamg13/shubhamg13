# PostCardly – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 19, 2026

PostCardly ("the App") lets you turn your photos into postcards. This policy explains what data is handled and how.

## 1. Photos & Messages

Your photos, filters, and messages are processed **entirely on your device**. We do not upload, store, or have access to your photos or postcard content.

## 2. Advertising

The App displays ads served by **Google AdMob**. Google may collect and use:

- Device identifiers (e.g., advertising ID)
- Approximate location
- Usage and diagnostic data

to serve and measure ads. This is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. Permissions

- **Camera** — used only to take photos for your postcards, when you grant permission.
- **Photo Library** — used only to select photos and save finished postcards, when you grant permission.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Ad-related data is handled by Google per its own privacy policy.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 6. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
